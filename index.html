About me

About Areti Chatzi

I am Areti Chatzi, a holistic therapist, reflexologist, Reiki master and yoga instructor.

Since 2010 I have run my holistic treatment studio in Skala, on the island of Patmos in Greece. The studio is open from May until mid-October. In winter I live in Athens and travel, giving treatments and inspiring people to live rich, meaningful lives through the art of healthy living.

My treatments aim to release muscle tension and headaches, invigorate overall well-being and support the mental and physical health of the people who trust me with their care.

Feel free to get in touch with questions or to book a treatment.

What I offer

Treatments

  • Massage

    Customised massage therapy designed uniquely for you. Whether you’re looking to release deep tension, relieve stress or restore balance, each treatment is crafted around your individual needs.

  • Reflexology

    Pressure on the reflex points of the feet to release tension and restore a sense of balance.

  • Face massage

    Works pressure points on the face, neck and shoulders. Relaxes the facial muscles and leaves the skin refreshed.

  • Sound massage

    Tune into deep relaxation. Sound massage offers a unique sensory journey where gentle sound waves guide your mind and body into a state of deep calm and balance.

  • Meditation

    Guided practice to quiet the mind and build awareness of the present moment.

I also offer Reiki and yoga, in the studio or privately. Ask me which treatment suits you.
